Zest Instructions

Zest is a simple pattern-matching card game in the spirit of SET, with a dynamic similar to war. This HTML version is for one player.

Each card has five fruits arranged in a circle. Each fruit is a strawberry, lemon, or bunch of blueberries.

A pair of cards is sweet if there is a pattern of three fruits appearing on both cards AND no pattern of four fruits appearing on both cards. Otherwise, the pair is tart. To count, patterns must have the same clockwise orientation.

Gameplay

Basic mode:
  1. You have 100 seconds to score as many points as possible.
  2. Correct answer: +1 point.
  3. Incorrect answer: −2 points.
  4. Five correct in a row: +15-second bonus.
  5. Keyboard shortcuts: S (Sweet), T (Tart), N (Next).
Challenge mode:
  1. For the first five points, gameplay is the same as in basic mode.
  2. Every five points, a new variant of the game is introduced: At 5 points, the cards rotate. At 10 points, dots flicker. At 15 points, an "inverse" variant is introduced, where the player identifies a missing fruit.
  3. After 20 points, the round type is chosen randomly from the four variants already introduced.
  4. At 10 points, the player receives a 60 second time bonus.

Notes

The two cards drawn are never the same, and cards with all fruits the same type are excluded. Pairs of cards are drawn at random, with each pair equally likely. The probability that a pair is a hit is approximately 50.5%. Thus, the chance of a pair being a hit or a miss is almost the same, and this is critical to the game dynamic.
